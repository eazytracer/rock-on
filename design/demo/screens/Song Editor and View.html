Rock On · Source · Song editor & song view

Add it fast, read it on stage.

Follows song-editor.md, song-view.md and workflow 04, in the B v2 look (grouped surfaces, tinted tuning pill, hybrid link tiles). The editor is a full-screen route with Cancel on the left and Save on the right, and the title is the only required field. Notes are deliberately not in the editor (spec Must not): they live in the song view and are edited in place.

How notes work

The song view shows every layer as its own labelled block. If you opened the song from a setlist, the set's note comes first, then Band notes (shared), then My notes (private, with a lock). The labels and icons match the row's notes mark, so the row tells you what you'll find (Mental Model).

Editing in place

Tap the pencil on a block and it becomes a Markdown field with a formatting bar above the keyboard: bold, italic, heading, list and checklist. Done saves. Band-note edits reach bandmates as the usual grouped banner ("Mike edited notes on Monkey Wrench"). You never leave the song (Flow).

Spotify, quietly

As you type a title, matches appear under the field. Tap one to fill title, artist, album and duration and add the Spotify link, or keep typing. If the lookup is slow or fails, the list stays empty and nothing you typed is lost (Postel's Law, Tesler's Law).

Pickers that fit a thumb

Tuning opens a sheet listing each tuning's colour and name, plus New tuning and Manage. Key uses a Circle of Fifths, majors outside and minors inside; tap any segment (the 4th phone works). Each segment is 40px or more (Fitts's Law, Hick's Law).
