Rock On · Source · Song row

Grouped, quieter.

Grouped sections, a tinted tuning pill, tuning → time → key → BPM, retune lines inside the group, no ⋯ button, notes mark after the title, and small link tiles. The View options sheet, sort and drag all work. Only the Tabs glyph is still open.

No kebab: tap, hold, swipe

Tap a row to open the song view, which holds every action. Swipe left for the two most common actions. Press and hold opens the full menu in the catalog. In a setlist it lifts the row to drag, and letting go without moving opens the menu. The title gets the whole first line (Occam's Razor), and these are the gestures people already use in iOS Mail and Android lists (Jakob's Law).

Notes, inside the title

A 15px grey mark right after the song name, read as part of the title rather than a button (Law of Proximity). It's always on, not a view option. It shows only when a song has notes, so no mark means no notes. A single sheet means band notes, a sheet with a lock means mine, and stacked sheets mean both. Tap the row to read them in the song view.

Hybrid link icons

The old app's subtle outlined tile, now 30px with a 16px glyph and a 44 hit area (Fitts's Law). The Spotify glyph is the soundwave from the Quick Links set. Tabs can be any of four glyphs; pick one in the study below and every row updates.
