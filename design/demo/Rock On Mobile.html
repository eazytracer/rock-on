Walk it through.

Every screen here is a locked phone design, not a redraw, so what you see is what we agreed. Tap through as you would in the app; anything the screen itself does (drag, timeline, tuner, metronome, chart controls, sheets) works as it did on its locked page. Go jumps to any check.

Run a whole jam: Jam Night is a working prototype (host, guest, bar TV, counter iPad) that shares one jam across browser windows.

Each screen keeps its locked page's sample data, so the song you open may differ from the row you tapped (e.g. the chart is Amazing Grace). Dark / light applies to kit screens; the four earliest locked pages (Song Row, Song Editor, Song Chart, Sound Tools) are dark only until they move onto the kit.
