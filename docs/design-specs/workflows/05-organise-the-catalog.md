# Workflow: Organise the catalog

**Goal:** find any song fast, see what you need at a glance, and keep the list tidy, without risking the band's work.

## Find

- Search over title, artist and album.
- Filters: tuning, upcoming show, tags. Active filters show as removable chips with **Clear all**.
- Sort: name, newest, oldest, next show.
- **View options** on the list choose the optional fields (key, BPM, artist, links, notes indicator, next show). They're saved for this list and follow you across devices.

## Look up

- Tapping a song opens the **song view**:
  - All fields and links.
  - Band notes and my notes.
  - Which setlists and upcoming shows use it.
- Editing is a separate, deliberate step: an **Edit** button in the song view.
- Link icons on the row open straight away (tabs, Spotify), with no need to open the song first.

## Tidy

- **Hide** (just for you): takes a band song out of your list without deleting it. **Show hidden** brings it back.
- **Delete:** the confirmation names the setlists and upcoming shows that use the song.
- **New version** instead of duplicating: see `explorations/song-versions.md`.

## Move between catalogs

- **Band → Personal:**
  - **Add to My Songs** makes a linked copy that follows the band version.
  - Your personal notes stay yours.
- **Personal → band:** **Add to ‹band›**, with a band picker if you're in several.
- **In Personal:** **"Songs in my bands that aren't in my catalog"**, with **Copy all** or pick some.
- Band → band directly isn't supported. Copy into Personal first.
- Personal songs show where they came from ("from ‹band›").

## Must not

- Duplicate songs (no "duplicate" action).
- Hide deleting behind a hidden-songs toggle, or make hiding look like deleting.
- Show the "next show" field outside the catalog.
