# Workflow: Host a jam (live or scheduled)

**Goal:** musicians in the same room find what they can all play, build a queue, and keep it afterwards.

## Flow

1. **Start:**
   - Jam → **Host a jam**: optional name, plus an optional starting setlist from your personal setlists.
   - Or schedule one on the calendar: at start time the host gets a reminder and a big **Go live** button.
2. **Share:** code, link and QR. Guests on the link see "Starting soon" until it's live.
3. **People arrive:**
   - Signed-in participants join by code. Their catalogs feed **Common songs**.
   - **Watchers** follow the public link with no account (optional name).
4. **Common songs:**
   - Songs at least two participants know.
   - Possible matches to confirm ("Yes, same song") or dismiss.
   - Shows "Waiting for others…" while you're alone.
5. **Build the setlist:**
   - The host adds from Common songs or their own catalog, reorders and removes.
   - Others see it read-only, live.
6. **End:**
   - **Save & end**: saved as a personal setlist.
   - Or **End without saving**.

## Must

- Common songs is the most prominent part of the session; it's what nothing else does.
- People: participants and watchers, with who's hosting.
- Active sessions show time left. Sessions expire on their own.
- The public view needs no account: live setlist, "updated Ns ago", a sign-up prompt. The native app opens it if it's installed.
- The jam hub lists your active, upcoming and joined sessions, and has **Join with a code**.

## Must not

- Start a scheduled jam automatically.
- Require watchers to sign up.
