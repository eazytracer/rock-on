# Workflow: Friends

**Goal:** connect with musicians you play with, so inviting them to events and jams is one tap, with control over who can find you.

## Flow

1. **Share yourself:** your friend code, with copy and QR.
2. **Find someone:**
   - Search by name (only people who are discoverable).
   - Enter their code.
   - Scan their QR. [Not built] (native camera)
3. **Request → accept:** they see **Requests (N)** and accept or decline. You see it under **Sent**.
4. **Use it:** invite friends to events from the friend picker.

## Must

- Privacy settings:
  - **Discoverable by name** on/off.
  - **Who can add you**: Everyone / Friends of friends / Code only.
- Lists: Requests, Sent, Friends. Remove a friend with confirmation.
- A badge for incoming requests (More, sidebar). [Future] Push for friend requests.

## Must not

- Show non-discoverable people in search.
- Show emails.
