# Workflow: Manage a band (roles, leave, delete)

**Goal:** the owner and admins can run the roster without fuss; anyone can leave cleanly and keep the songs they learned.

## Roles

- **Owner:**
  - Make or remove admins.
  - **Transfer ownership**: type a confirmation; the old owner becomes an admin.
  - Delete the band.
- **Admins:**
  - Edit band info (name, description).
  - Invite controls; see emails.
  - Remove members, but not other admins.
- **Everyone:** edit their own instruments (primary starred), leave the band.

## Leave a band [Not built]

1. Band Members → **Leave band**.
2. The prompt explains:
   - Band songs, setlists and band notes stay with the band.
   - Your linked copies become your own songs.
   - Offers **Also copy every band song I don't have yet** to My Songs.
3. Confirm. You land in Personal (or your next band), and the band disappears from the switcher.

- The owner must transfer ownership first, or delete the band.

## Delete a band [Not built]

- Owner only.
- Type the band name to confirm.
- Members are warned what they'll lose and offered the same copy-to-My-Songs option.

## Must

- Member list: search, role badges, instruments, joined date. Emails for admins only.
- Tapping a member shows their details; tapping your own row opens your profile.
- Every role change and removal asks for confirmation and names the person.

## Must not

- Let the last owner leave without a successor.
- Delete a member's personal songs or notes when they leave or are removed.
