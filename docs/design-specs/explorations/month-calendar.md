# Exploration: Month calendar

**Idea:** a month grid alongside the agenda. Design it now; it ships with calendar sync (.ics, Google/Apple), RSVP and recurring practices.

**Mockups should show**

1. Shows, practices and events told apart at a glance in a small cell, by type and not only by colour.
2. Confirmed vs unconfirmed shows; cancelled items.
3. Tapping a day on a phone gives that day's agenda.
4. Room for availability ("who can make it") later.
5. Switching between agenda and month, remembered per user.
