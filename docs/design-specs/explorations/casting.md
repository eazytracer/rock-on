# Exploration: Casting (Pro)

**Idea:** decide who plays which part on each song. It's for large, rotating groups (School of Rock–style programs, a dozen members swapping in and out), not the average garage band. It's likely a Pro-plan feature with a **band director** role.

**Settled**

- Off by default. When it's off, no parts, slots or cast progress appear anywhere.
- Songs carry their own parts (Lead guitar, Rhythm guitar, Harmony vocals, Harmonica…), defaulting from the band's instruments.
- Skill isn't rated; it's expressed through parts ("lead" vs "rhythm").
- Today, events cast with fixed parts (vocals, guitar, bass, drums, keys, other) in a list per song or a song × part grid. Guests raise hands; managers assign.

**Questions the mockups must answer**

1. Where does casting live for a band: on the setlist, the show, or both?
2. How do you cast a 20-song set for 12 people without it feeling cumbersome? Previous mockups always did.
3. How does a member see "my songs tonight" at a glance?

**Must not:** add any weight to bands that don't use it.
