# Sign in / Sign up

**Purpose:** get in with as little friction as possible (`workflows/01`).

**Must**

- **Apple** [Not built], **Google**, email and password (show/hide).
- Sign up: display name, email, password, confirm → "Check your email".
- **Forgot password** that works [Not built].
- Carries the link you arrived from through sign-in, and returns you to it.
- Messages: signed out, session expired, wrong credentials, cancelled social sign-in.

**Must not:** test or mock-user panels; dead links.
