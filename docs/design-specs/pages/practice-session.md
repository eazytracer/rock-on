# Practice session

**Purpose:** full screen, song by song, during practice. It's well liked on desktop/TV; keep that behaviour.

**Must**

- Song X of N; elapsed timer; progress indicator.
- Big title; key, BPM, tuning (colour plus name), duration.
- Band notes, scrollable, with adjustable text size.
- **Up next**, with a **tuning-change alert**.
- Prev / Next / End.
- Arrow keys, Home/End, Esc; **Bluetooth page-turner pedals**.
- Layouts: TV, tablet landscape, tablet portrait, phone. Cycle them by hand.
- Phone:
  - Portrait first; swipe between songs, with big Prev/Next buttons as well.
  - Rotating uses the tablet layout.
- Keep the screen awake; works offline.

**Must not:** small or precise controls; anything that dims or locks mid-song.
