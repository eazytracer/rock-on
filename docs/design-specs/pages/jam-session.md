# Jam session (live)

**Purpose:** the live jam (`workflows/09`).

**Must**

- Session name, code; **Share**: copy link, QR.
- **People:** participants (host marked) and watchers. Collapsible on a phone.
- **Common songs** (the most prominent part):
  - Songs at least two participants know.
  - Confirm or dismiss possible matches; add to the setlist.
  - Shows "Waiting for others…" while you're alone.
- **Setlist:**
  - The host adds from Common songs or their catalog, reorders, removes.
  - Others see it read-only, live.
- Host menu: Save as setlist, Refresh matches, End.
- End: **Save & end** / **End without saving** / Cancel. Afterwards: start a new jam, or open the saved setlist.

**Future:** [Future] now-playing pointer ("you're up next on bass").
