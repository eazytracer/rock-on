# More (phone)

**Purpose:** everything outside the four main tabs.

**Must**

- **Band card:** name, up to 5 member avatars, member count, **Manage band** → Band Members. In Personal: "Create or join a band".
- **You:** name plus your role in the current band, or "Personal" → Profile.
- Jam, Events, Friends (request badge), Band Members.
- Settings, Help & feedback, Sign out.
- The version comes from the build (never hardcoded).
