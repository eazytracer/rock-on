# Band Members

**Purpose:** the band's roster and invites (`workflows/02`, `03`, `12`).

**Must**

- Band name, description, member count, created date; **Edit band info** (admins).
- **Invite** (admins): link, QR, code; copy, share, **Regenerate** (warns the old one stops working).
- Members:
  - Search; owner first, then admins.
  - Name, role badge, instruments (★ primary), joined date; email for admins only.
- Member menu:
  - Edit instruments (self or admin).
  - Make or remove admin, transfer ownership (owner).
  - Remove (owner; admins can remove members but not admins).
- Your own row → your profile.
- **Leave band** (everyone) [Not built]; **Delete band** (owner) [Not built].

**Must not:** emails for non-admins; invite controls for non-admins.

**States:** a band of one → a prominent invite card.

**Future:** [Future] email invites; [Future] Pro-plan band director role.
