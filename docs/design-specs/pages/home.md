# Home

**Purpose:** what's next for me and my band.

**Must**

- New-user / new-band walkthrough, dismissable, ticking itself off (`workflows/02`).
- **Next show** card: name, Confirmed, countdown, date and time, venue, set (song count), pay.
- **Next practice** card: title or date, countdown, time, location, songs to practise.
- **Upcoming events:** the next 3 (jam nights, scheduled jams); "View all" → Calendar.
- Quick actions: Add song, New setlist, Schedule practice, Book show. In Personal: Add song, Host an event, Find friends.

**Must not**

- Stats tiles (song, setlist and show counts).
- Dead ends: every empty card offers its action ("No practice scheduled — plan one").

**Roles / context**

- Personal: no show or practice cards; a "Create or join a band" card instead.

**Links:** cards → the show, practice or event; quick actions → their create flows.
